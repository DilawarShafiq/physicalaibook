# Module 1: The EHR Ecosystem

*History, Vendors & CEHRS Exam Blueprint*

**Duration:** 4 hours · **Level:** Foundational · **Lessons:** 4

Understand where electronic health records came from, who the major players are, and exactly what the NHA CEHRS exam expects you to know.

## Prerequisites

None — this is an entry point into the book.

:::info Learning outcomes

By the end of this module you will be able to:

- Trace the evolution from paper records to interoperable EHR systems
- Identify the major EHR vendors and their market positions
- Decode the CEHRS exam blueprint: domains, weights, and passing criteria
:::
