# Module 8: Interoperability & Data Exchange

*HL7 FHIR, USCDI, HIEs & Patient Data Portability*

**Duration:** 4 hours · **Level:** Intermediate · **Lessons:** 3

Health data must flow. Understand the standards, regulations, and technology enabling interoperability — and why the 21st Century Cures Act made information blocking a federal offense.

## Prerequisites

- [Module 1: The EHR Ecosystem](../module-01-ehr-ecosystem/index.md)

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this module you will be able to:

- Explain HL7 FHIR R4 and its role in modern health data exchange
- Apply USCDI requirements to data completeness assessments
- Describe HIE models and appropriate use cases for each
