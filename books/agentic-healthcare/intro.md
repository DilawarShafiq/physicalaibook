# AI Healthcare Employees

### Building Personal Medical Billers at Scale

> US healthcare spends $500B/year on admin paperwork. One engineer with the right agent framework can automate what a team of 20 used to do manually.

Build, deploy, and scale AI agents that automate US healthcare administration. RCM, prior auth, medical coding, denial management, and the complete Personal Medical Biller — on the Autosapien stack.

**Modules:** 8 · **Lessons:** 22 · **Total hours:** 52+ · **Author:** Dilawar Gopang

## Who this book is for

- AI Developers
- Healthcare Startups
- RCM Professionals
- Practice Managers
- Health IT Engineers

## Modules

### [Module 1: The US Healthcare Admin Crisis](./module-01-us-healthcare-admin-crisis/index.md)

*$500B Problem, Infinite Opportunity* — US healthcare wastes more on administration than most countries spend on healthcare entirely. Understand the scale of the problem before you build the solution.

`3 hours` · `Foundational` · 3 lessons

### [Module 2: Eligibility & Prior Auth Agents](./module-02-eligibility-prior-auth-agents/index.md)

*Automating the Front Door of the Revenue Cycle* — Insurance verification and prior authorization are the highest-volume, most automatable tasks in healthcare admin. Build agents that eliminate phone calls, faxes, and manual portal entry.

`6 hours` · `Intermediate` · 3 lessons

### [Module 3: AI Medical Coding & CDI](./module-03-ai-medical-coding/index.md)

*ICD-10, CPT & Clinical Documentation Intelligence* — Medical coding is the translation of clinical documentation into billable codes. AI achieves 95%+ accuracy on high-volume, well-documented encounters. Build coding agents that never miss a secondary diagnosis or a modifier.

`6 hours` · `Advanced` · 3 lessons

### [Module 4: Claims Submission & Denial Management AI](./module-04-claims-denial-management/index.md)

*Clean Claims, Smart Appeals & Automated A/R Follow-Up* — A claim denied is revenue at risk. Build agents that submit clean claims the first time, auto-appeal standard denials with clinical evidence, and work the A/R until every dollar is collected.

`6 hours` · `Advanced` · 3 lessons

### [Module 5: The Personal Medical Biller](./module-05-personal-medical-biller/index.md)

*Building a Digital FTE for Healthcare Billing* — A complete AI agent that acts as a patient's dedicated billing advocate — and a practice's full-stack RCM employee. Architecture, orchestration, memory, and deployment of the Personal Medical Biller.

`7 hours` · `Advanced` · 3 lessons

### [Module 6: HIPAA-Compliant AI Agent Deployment](./module-06-hipaa-compliant-ai-deployment/index.md)

*Architecture, BAAs, Audit Trails & Production Safety* — Every AI agent that touches PHI in US healthcare must be HIPAA-compliant. This is not optional and it is not just a policy — it is architecture. Build it right from day one.

`5 hours` · `Advanced` · 3 lessons

### [Module 7: Deploying at Scale: Multi-Tenant SaaS](./module-07-scale-deployment-saas/index.md)

*From One Practice to Thousands — Architecture for Scale* — Building a Personal Medical Biller for one practice is a proof of concept. Deploying it for 10,000 practices is a business. The architecture decisions that separate these two are made here.

`5 hours` · `Advanced` · 2 lessons

### [Module 8: Building on the Autosapien Stack](./module-08-autosapien-stack/index.md)

*xEHR.io, Agent SMITH & the RCM Automation Platform* — Autosapien's healthcare products are the reference implementation for everything in this book. This module shows how xEHR.io, Agent SMITH, and the RCM Automation platform connect to deliver a complete Personal Medical Biller.

`4 hours` · `Foundational` · 2 lessons
