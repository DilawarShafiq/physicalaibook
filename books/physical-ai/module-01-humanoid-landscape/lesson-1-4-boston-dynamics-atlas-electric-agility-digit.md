# Boston Dynamics Atlas (Electric) & Agility Digit

**Duration:** 40 min · **Level:** Foundational · **Module:** 1. The Humanoid Landscape · **Tags:** `boston-dynamics`, `atlas`, `agility`, `digit`

## Overview

Boston Dynamics retired its hydraulic Atlas in April 2024 and introduced a fully electric successor — quieter, stronger, and more suitable for commercial deployment. Simultaneously, Agility Robotics (owned by Hyundai) began mass production of Digit at its RoboFab facility in Oregon, the first humanoid robot factory.

## Key Insights

- Electric Atlas (April 2024): no published specs yet, but BD claims it is stronger and has greater range of motion than hydraulic Atlas
- Hyundai partnership: Atlas targeted at Hyundai/Kia automotive manufacturing lines — spot welding, parts handling, quality inspection
- Agility Digit: 1.75m, 65kg, designed for logistics; deployed at Amazon fulfillment centers doing tote handling since 2023
- RoboFab (Salem, OR): capacity for 10,000 Digit units/year — first humanoid-scale mass production facility in North America
- Digit uses a bespoke MPC-based locomotion stack with learned policies for manipulation; legs fold for confined spaces
- Key difference from Figure/Tesla: both BD and Agility prioritize task-specific deployment over general-purpose learning
