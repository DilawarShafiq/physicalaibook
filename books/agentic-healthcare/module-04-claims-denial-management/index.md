# Module 4: Claims Submission & Denial Management AI

*Clean Claims, Smart Appeals & Automated A/R Follow-Up*

**Duration:** 6 hours · **Level:** Advanced · **Lessons:** 3

A claim denied is revenue at risk. Build agents that submit clean claims the first time, auto-appeal standard denials with clinical evidence, and work the A/R until every dollar is collected.

## Prerequisites

- [Module 3: AI Medical Coding & CDI](../module-03-ai-medical-coding/index.md)

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this module you will be able to:

- Build a pre-submission claim scrubbing agent that catches edits before payer rejection
- Automate standard denial appeals with clinical evidence retrieval
- Design an A/R follow-up agent with payer-specific rule logic
