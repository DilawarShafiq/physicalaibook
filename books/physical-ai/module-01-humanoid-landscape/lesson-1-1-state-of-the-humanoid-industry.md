# State of the Humanoid Industry

**Duration:** 40 min · **Level:** Foundational · **Module:** 1. The Humanoid Landscape · **Tags:** `industry`, `market`, `strategy`

## Overview

Humanoid robotics crossed a credibility threshold in 2023-2024. Major manufacturers moved from lab demos to factory deployments, venture capital topped $1B in single-round raises, and Goldman Sachs revised its humanoid market forecast to $38B by 2035. This lesson maps the landscape you are competing in.

## Key Insights

- Goldman Sachs (2023) projects 250,000 humanoid units shipped annually by 2030, rising to 1.4M by 2035 — a $38B market
- Tesla, Figure, 1X, Apptronik, Agility, and Unitree all reached deployable prototypes between 2023-2025
- The dominant business model is "robots as a service" (RaaS), not hardware sales — Figure charges ~$100k/year per unit
- Amazon, BMW, and Hyundai are the first anchor customers, providing real-world training data in exchange for early access
- Key technology convergence: electric QDD actuators + transformer-based vision-language-action models made 2024 the breakthrough year
- China (Unitree, UBTECH, Fourier) is targeting the $20k-50k researcher and SME market; US companies are targeting $100k+ enterprise contracts
