# Module 3: Health Record Documentation

*Legal Records, Clinical Document Types & Chart Completion*

**Duration:** 6 hours · **Level:** Intermediate · **Lessons:** 4

22% of the CEHRS exam — the highest-weight domain. Know every document type, who is responsible for completing it, and what makes a record legally defensible.

## Prerequisites

- [Module 1: The EHR Ecosystem](../module-01-ehr-ecosystem/index.md)

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this module you will be able to:

- Identify the legal health record and its components in any care setting
- Distinguish between all major clinical document types and their requirements
- Manage chart deficiencies and delinquency according to accreditation standards
