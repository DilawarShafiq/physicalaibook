# Tesla Optimus — Scale as Moat

**Duration:** 40 min · **Level:** Foundational · **Module:** 1. The Humanoid Landscape · **Tags:** `tesla`, `optimus`, `hardware`, `scale`

## Overview

Tesla's approach to humanoid robotics is inseparable from its manufacturing infrastructure. Optimus Gen 2 (December 2023) benefits from Tesla's custom silicon (Dojo, FSD chip), its in-house actuator manufacturing at scale, and the world's largest fleet of real-world robot deployment data from Tesla vehicles. The strategy: use the factory as the training environment.

## Key Insights

- Optimus Gen 2 weighs 57kg (11kg less than Gen 1), walks 30% faster, and has improved 22-DOF hands with tactile sensing on all fingers
- Uses Tesla's custom FSD (Full Self-Driving) chip for on-board inference — the same silicon that processes 1,000+ TOPS in Tesla vehicles
- Hand dexterity goal: handle eggs without breaking them, thread a needle — demonstrated publicly in late 2023 demos
- Elon Musk projects 1,000 Optimus units in Tesla factories by end of 2025, scaling to "millions" by 2030
- Key advantage: Tesla manufactures its own actuators, reducing BOM cost by ~40% vs. buying off-the-shelf; projects sub-$20k at volume
- Autopilot neural network architecture directly ported to robot perception — same occupancy network approach used in FSD
