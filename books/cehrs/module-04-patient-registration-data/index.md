# Module 4: Patient Registration & Data Management

*MPI, Demographics, Consent & Data Quality*

**Duration:** 5 hours · **Level:** Intermediate · **Lessons:** 4

Every patient encounter begins with registration. Errors here cascade through clinical, billing, and legal systems. Master the MPI, data quality standards, and consent management.

## Prerequisites

- [Module 1: The EHR Ecosystem](../module-01-ehr-ecosystem/index.md)

:::info Learning outcomes

By the end of this module you will be able to:

- Manage the Master Patient Index and prevent duplicate/overlay records
- Apply data quality standards across all demographic and clinical data
- Process consent forms, advance directives, and patient rights documentation
:::
