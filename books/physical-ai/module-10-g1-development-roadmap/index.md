# Module 10: G1 Development Roadmap

*Architecture, Milestones & 2030 Strategy*

**Duration:** 4 hours · **Level:** Foundational · **Lessons:** 4

Synthesize everything. Define G1's technology stack, resolve the key architectural decisions, and lay out the concrete development roadmap from 2026 to commercial launch.

## Prerequisites

- [Module 1: The Humanoid Landscape](../module-01-humanoid-landscape/index.md)

:::info Learning outcomes

By the end of this module you will be able to:

- Specify G1's complete technology stack across all subsystems
- Identify the 5 highest-risk technical challenges and mitigation plans
- Define 2026-2030 development milestones with measurable criteria
:::
