# Module 2: Medical Terminology & Body Systems

*Word Parts, Clinical Language & High-Yield Abbreviations*

**Duration:** 6 hours · **Level:** Foundational · **Lessons:** 5

21% of the CEHRS exam. Master word construction rules, body system terminology, and the abbreviations that appear in clinical documentation every day.

## Prerequisites

- [Module 1: The EHR Ecosystem](../module-01-ehr-ecosystem/index.md)

:::info Learning outcomes

By the end of this module you will be able to:

- Decode any medical term using prefix/suffix/root rules
- Apply correct terminology for all major body systems
- Recognize high-yield abbreviations tested on the CEHRS exam
:::
