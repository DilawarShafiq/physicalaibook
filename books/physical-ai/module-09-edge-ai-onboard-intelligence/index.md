# Module 9: Edge AI & On-Board Intelligence

*NVIDIA Orin, Neuromorphic Chips & Real-Time Inference*

**Duration:** 6 hours · **Level:** Advanced · **Lessons:** 3

G1 cannot depend on the cloud. Build a complete on-board AI stack that runs foundation models, locomotion controllers, and safety monitors within the compute and power budget of a mobile humanoid.

## Prerequisites

- [Module 5: Foundation Models & VLA Architecture](../module-05-foundation-models-vla/index.md)

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this module you will be able to:

- Design the compute architecture for on-board VLA inference
- Apply quantization and model compression for edge deployment
- Understand neuromorphic computing for ultra-low power sensing
