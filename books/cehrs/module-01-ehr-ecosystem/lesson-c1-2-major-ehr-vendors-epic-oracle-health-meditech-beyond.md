# Major EHR Vendors: Epic, Oracle Health, MEDITECH & Beyond

## Overview

Six vendors control over 80% of the US hospital EHR market. As a CEHRS specialist, you will encounter these systems throughout your career. Understanding their strengths, typical clients, and core modules is essential for both the exam and practical work.

## Key concepts

:::note Key idea

Epic Systems: ~35% market share in US hospitals; dominates large academic medical centers (Mayo Clinic, Cleveland Clinic, Kaiser Permanente); known for MyChart patient portal and strong interoperability via Care Everywhere

:::

- Oracle Health (formerly Cerner): ~25% market share; strong in mid-size hospitals and federal health (VA, DoD after $10B DHMSM contract); uses HealtheIntent population health platform
- MEDITECH: dominant in critical access hospitals and smaller community hospitals; MEDITECH Expanse (cloud-based) is their current platform; ~15% market share
- Athenahealth: strongest in physician practices and ambulatory settings; cloud-native, subscription model; ~10% of ambulatory market; known for revenue cycle integration
- Allscripts/Veradigm: primarily physician practices; declining market share after major hospital clients left; stronger in specialty practices
- EHR module structure: all major systems divide into clinical (physician, nursing, pharmacy), administrative (scheduling, registration), and financial (billing, coding, claims) modules
