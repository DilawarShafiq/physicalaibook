# Module 7: Simulation & Digital Twins

*NVIDIA Isaac Sim, MuJoCo & Sim-to-Real*

**Duration:** 6 hours · **Level:** Intermediate · **Lessons:** 3

Train G1's locomotion and manipulation policies in simulation before deployment. Build physics-accurate digital twins and master the sim-to-real transfer techniques that make it work.

## Prerequisites

- [Module 3: Bipedal Locomotion & Whole-Body Control](../module-03-bipedal-locomotion/index.md)

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this module you will be able to:

- Set up a complete G1 simulation in Isaac Sim
- Apply domain randomization for robust sim-to-real transfer
- Train locomotion and manipulation policies at scale using Isaac Lab
