# Module 5: HIPAA Privacy Rule

*PHI, Patient Rights & the Minimum Necessary Standard*

**Duration:** 6 hours · **Level:** Intermediate · **Lessons:** 4

Healthcare's foundational privacy law. Know the 18 PHI identifiers, all six patient rights, the minimum necessary standard, and exactly when you can and cannot share health information.

## Prerequisites

- [Module 1: The EHR Ecosystem](../module-01-ehr-ecosystem/index.md)

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this module you will be able to:

- Identify all 18 PHI identifiers and apply de-identification rules
- Explain all six HIPAA patient rights and how to fulfill each one
- Apply the minimum necessary standard to real-world disclosure scenarios
